Turning validation warnings into action

Today, critical and routine warnings look the same and share one easy-to-dismiss prompt, so users and agents often proceed past a warning that will cause deployment to fail. This explores separating critical, likely-to-fail issues from routine warnings with clearer severity, structure, and prompts, so people pay attention and act instead of dismissing them.
